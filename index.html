About Our Shelter

At Koro, we believe every animal deserves a safe haven filled with love and comfort. Our name comes from the Esperanto word for “heart”, because that’s what we hope every rescued pet finds: a home that opens its heart to them.

Whether you’re looking for an energetic companion for morning walks or a gentle friend for quiet evenings, your new family member is waiting for you.
